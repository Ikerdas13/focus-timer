## Descripción completa — ENGLISH (pegar en la Chrome Web Store)

```
Do you lose focus switching between tabs to check the time? Focus Timer is a floating Pomodoro timer and study clock that stays visible on every website — always there, never in the way.

Built on the Pomodoro Technique: 25 minutes of deep work, 5-minute break, repeat. Proven to reduce procrastination, improve concentration, and make your study and work sessions more productive.

★ KEY FEATURES

• Floating widget — drag it to any corner of any website, any size
• Synced across all tabs — one timer running everywhere, no duplicates
• Study clock — live time display in 12h or 24h format right on the widget
• Custom colors — set independent colors for the menu, work sessions and break sessions
• 3 widget sizes — S / M / L, including a minimal circle mode
• Custom opacity — control how visible the widget is when you're not hovering
• Custom background image — personalize with your own photo
• Real-time study stats — daily, weekly and monthly minutes tracked automatically
• Site blocker — block distracting domains (YouTube, Reddit…) during work sessions
• Long break — automatic 15–30 min rest after every 4 sessions (classic Pomodoro cycle)
• Desktop notifications — get alerted when work or break ends, even in the background
• Sound alarm — optional bell when your session changes
• Bilingual — switch between Spanish and English from the widget itself

★ PERFECT FOR

Students pulling long study sessions, developers in deep work mode, writers on a deadline, remote workers who need structure. Works great alongside study-with-me videos, coding sprints, exam prep, and any task that benefits from focused time blocks.

★ 100% PRIVATE

All data lives in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. No accounts. No tracking. No analytics.

Designed with care by Iker G.
```
