★ Key features

Everything you need to stay focused

Built on the proven Pomodoro Technique. 25 min work, 5 min rest, repeat.

🎯

Floating on every tab

The timer stays visible as you browse. Drag it to any corner — it snaps in place automatically.

🔔

Notifications & alarm

Desktop notifications and an optional sound alarm when your session ends, even with the tab in the background.

🏖️

Long break every 4 sessions

Classic Pomodoro cycle: after 4 work sessions, a longer rest (15–30 min). Configurable and optional.

⚡

Synced across all tabs

One timer runs everywhere. Pause in one tab, see it paused in all others — in real time.

Study stats

Daily, weekly and monthly study minutes tracked automatically. No setup needed.

Spanish & English

Switch between languages directly from the widget. Preference saved across all tabs.
