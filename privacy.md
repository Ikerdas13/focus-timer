Focus Timer — Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Focus Timer does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

All data stored by this extension (timer state, settings, background 
image, study statistics) is saved exclusively in the user's local 
browser using chrome.storage.local. This data never leaves the device 
and is not accessible to the developer or any third party.

No analytics. No tracking. No external services.
